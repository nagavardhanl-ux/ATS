    Frequently asked questions

    What is an AI recruiting agent? +

    An AI recruiting agent does the volume work of agency recruitment on its own: sourcing candidates, reading resumes, ranking them against the client requirement, running first-round interviews and collecting the details a CV does not carry. Crewtide is an AI recruiting agent built on the AIQoD platform. A recruiter reviews the results and decides who goes to the client.

    Who is Crewtide for? +

    Recruitment and staffing agencies. It is built for agency owners, delivery heads and recruiters who work several client mandates at once. It is not an in-house HR tool; a separate product for HR teams is planned.

    Does Crewtide work with our ATS? +

    Yes. Crewtide can be integrated with your existing ATS or recruitment CRM. Candidate records, stages and tracking stay where they are. Nothing is replaced or migrated.

    How does the AI avatar interviewer work? +

    Shortlisted candidates are interviewed by an AI avatar. The AI arranges the interview with the candidate directly, so there is no scheduling back and forth. Questions are built from the client requirement, every answer is captured and assessed, and the result is shown next to the resume score for the recruiter to review.

    How is an AI avatar interview different from a one-way video interview? +

    In a one-way video interview, candidates record answers and someone still has to watch every recording. The AI avatar runs the interview and returns an assessment, so recruiters review results instead of hours of video.

    Does Crewtide help us submit to clients faster? +

    That is the point of it. It reads, ranks, interviews and collects candidate details for every open mandate in parallel, so a client-ready shortlist is out before a competing agency gets there. In agency work, the first strong submission usually wins the placement.

    How does Crewtide handle high-volume and seasonal mandates? +

    Capacity scales with demand. When several mandates land together, the agents work them in parallel. When it goes quiet, nothing sits idle, and consumption pricing means cost follows the work done.

    Does it collect candidate details like CTC and notice period? +

    Yes. Current CTC, expected CTC, notice period and availability are rarely on a CV and usually hold up a submission. Crewtide reaches out to each shortlisted candidate and collects them, so the profile is ready to submit.

    Where does Crewtide source candidates from? +

    LinkedIn, the other channels you connect, bulk uploads in PDF, DOCX and TXT, and your existing candidate database.

    How is the match score calculated? +

    Against the client requirement, using weights your team sets per role across skills, experience and education. The skills that drove the score are shown with every result, so you can explain a submission when a client asks.

    Do recruiters still make the decisions? +

    Yes. Crewtide prepares the shortlist, the interview assessment and the candidate details. A recruiter approves who goes to the client at every stage.

    How is Crewtide priced? +

    On consumption. Cost follows the mandates worked and resumes processed, so you are not paying for idle capacity between surges. Resumes are pre-processed in layers before an AI model reads them, so cost does not rise in step with volume.

    How long does it take to go live? +

    The first configuration goes live in a day, with measurable results typically inside one to two weeks.
